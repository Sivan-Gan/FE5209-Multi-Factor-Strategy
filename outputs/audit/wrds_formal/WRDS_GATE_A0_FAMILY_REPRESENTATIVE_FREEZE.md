# Formal Gate A0 Family-Representative Freeze

## 1. Gate A0 purpose

Gate A0 determines how each economic family is represented. It does not determine whether a family belongs in the Final Factor Set.

## 2. Governance lineage

The frozen registry and within-family validation at commit `f18e4c2c491c355bb7f05e9c61fdc0eac3414598` provide the evidence lineage. This document records the subsequent approved human decision.

## 3. Human decision status

**GATE A0 — FROZEN.** This is a recorded human decision, not an automated score, ordering, or model output.

## 4. Value decision

`VALUE_CURRENT` is retained. Eligible challengers did not establish a sufficiently clear, economically coherent, multi-dimensional improvement over the incumbent. VALUE_EP showed some IC-stability advantages, but tradable portfolio evidence did not establish clear superiority; VALUE_BP and VALUE_EP_BP were weaker.

## 5. Quality decision

`QUALITY_CURRENT` is retained. QUALITY_CURRENT provided the strongest overall eligible-representative evidence in the completed human review. QUALITY_ROE and QUALITY_PROFITABILITY did not establish a coherent advantage.

## 6. Momentum decision

`MOMENTUM_12_1` replaces `MOMENTUM_CURRENT`. MOMENTUM_12_1 showed a clear, economically coherent, multi-dimensional advantage over MOMENTUM_CURRENT and MOMENTUM_6_1. The advantage covered 1W/4W IC, stability, gross spread evidence, implementation economics, and common-within-family evidence.

## 7. Low-Risk decision

`LOWRISK_CURRENT` is retained. LOWRISK_VOL had modestly higher IC point estimates but did not establish clear multi-dimensional superiority over the incumbent. The specifications are highly correlated in score and spread-return space, while LOWRISK_CURRENT retained competitive portfolio evidence.

## 8. Investment / Dividend / Size passthrough

`INVESTMENT_CURRENT`, `DIVIDEND_CURRENT`, and `SIZE_CURRENT` proceed unchanged because each is a single-specification family. No internal comparison occurred.

## 9. Component-diagnostic treatment

`VALUE_FCF_COMPONENT`, `QUALITY_GPA_COMPONENT`, `QUALITY_NEG_LEVERAGE_COMPONENT`, and `QUALITY_ROA_COMPONENT` remain diagnostic-only. None is promoted to a whole-market family representative.

## 10. Incumbent-default application

The approved human review applied the frozen incumbent-default rule. Momentum was the only multi-specification family for which the documented evidence justified replacement.

## 11. Why Momentum justified incumbent replacement

The approved rationale records consistent 1W/4W forward-IC, stability, gross spread, implementation and common-sample evidence for `MOMENTUM_12_1`.

## 12. Why no other incumbent was replaced

For Value, Quality, and Low Risk, the human review found no sufficiently clear, coherent, multi-dimensional case to overcome the incumbent-default rule.

## 14. Adaptive-research disclosure

Gate A0 is adaptive within the known 2011–2015 Factor Selection evidence because it was introduced after incumbent evidence had been reviewed. The period is not labelled untouched for this design question.

## 15. Protected later-period samples

No 2016–2020, 2021–2025, or 2026 performance was accessed for this decision. Those samples remain protected and are not marked contaminated.

## 16. Round-2 challenger status

**ROUND 2 — NOT AUTHORIZED.** FCF-based Value and GP/A-focused Quality remain future-research hypotheses only. No RMW, RMWc, Trend Momentum, Low Beta, BAB, Shareholder Yield, Net Payout Yield, EV-based Value, or other new definition is authorized.

## 17. Difference between Gate A0 and Gate A

Gate A0 freezes one representation per economic family. Gate A remains pending and will decide whether each frozen family enters the Final Factor Set.
